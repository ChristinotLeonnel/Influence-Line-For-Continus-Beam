# Prompts Google Veo — Tsaraloha.LIPoutreContinue

Guide de prompts text-to-video pour générer une vidéo de présentation moderne via Google Veo.

---

## Directives Générales de Style
* **Esthétique** : High-tech, cinématique, photoréaliste mélangé à des hologrammes d'ingénierie (HUD).
* **Palette** : Tons sombres (bleu nuit, noir), néons vert menthe (`#4CE8A0`) et jaune d'or (`#FFCC44`).
* **Caméra** : Mouvements lents, travellings fluides, macrophotographie et grand angle.

---

## Séquence des Prompts (Scène par Scène)

### Scène 1 : L'Introduction (Ingénierie)
> **Prompt Veo** :
> `Cinematic wide shot of a modern concrete multi-span bridge over a misty canyon at dusk. Futuristic glowing blue and green structural analysis lines (holographic HUD) overlay the bridge spans, showing tension vectors, load points, and bending moment diagrams. Slow camera tracking forward, 4k, photorealistic, Unreal Engine 5 render style.`

### Scène 2 : Le Code & L'Architecture
> **Prompt Veo** :
> `Macro close-up shot of a dark-themed computer screen with clean, glowing Python code scrolling. The camera pans smoothly across the text. Neon green and cyan syntax highlighting. Out of focus background showing a modern engineering office with blueprint displays, high-tech interface, soft ambient glow, 35mm lens.`

### Scène 3 : La Déformation & Ligne d'Influence
> **Prompt Veo** :
> `3D animation of a steel beam bending dynamically under a moving glowing golden point load. An elegant dark-themed line chart (influence line) fades into the air above the beam, tracing a smooth sine-like curve in green and blue. The area under the curve fills with glowing green gridlines. Smooth camera orbit, architectural visualization, clean interface design.`

### Scène 4 : Les Charges Mobiles (Convoi + UDL)
> **Prompt Veo** :
> `Split screen infographics. On one side, a heavy truck convoy (represented by red neon arrow vectors) moves across a bridge model. On the other side, a multi-segment distributed load (represented by segmented green hatched boxes) highlights the active influence zones. Sharp labels and coordinates pop up, futuristic UI, technical look.`

### Scène 5 : Conclusion & Outro
> **Prompt Veo** :
> `Clean high-tech outro. A glowing 3D wireframe model of a continuous beam collapses into a sleek minimal logo "TSARALOHA" in silver and cyan lettering. The subtitle "LIPoutreContinue" fades in below. Deep blue background with subtle floating grid particles, elegant, professional branding video.`
